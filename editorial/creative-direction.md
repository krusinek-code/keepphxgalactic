# Creative direction — Compilation 001

Keep PHX Galactic is an experiential Phoenix cultural archive, not a directory. Tagline: **Stay curious. Find your strange.**

## Visual direction
Cosmic dark backgrounds, vivid pink, neon green, desert sunset oranges, violet and turquoise; retro-futurist, playful, archival. The website mockup is inspiration, not an active build.

## Stories
Serialized Instagram carousels with independent yet connected posts. Recovered lore (documented history and reported folklore) is separate from original fiction. Candidate series: Phoenix Lights (sighting, radio broadcast, response, cultural afterlife), Dreamy Draw (place, mining history, alien legend, evidence), Midnight Transmissions (Art Bell and regional radio folklore).

## Locations and people to investigate
Ziggy's / Stardust Pinbar; user-mentioned Starbust Pinball Bar (name and location unverified); Dreamy Draw; Grand Avenue; Galactic Challenge storefront in a Chandler mall. Artist prospects: John Arvizu (space-woman artwork), Danny.et. Obtain permissions and verify specifics before publication.

## Stickers and artifacts
Sticker Collection 001: desert/alien/oddities concepts; possible lyric sticker **“What can I say? I have taste.”**, attributed to Phoenix Galactic, *Ghost of Judgement*, interlude *I Have Taste*. The physical sticker may reference a digital artifact ID in the future.

## Records
Keep PHX Galactic Records is an umbrella imprint. Immediate project: **French Elevator Music Library**, generic lounge/easy-listening production music, released in volumes. Separate future project: **The Melodrama Archives**, theatrical radio drama/operatic music, potentially connected to story posts.

Proposed French Elevator Music Library volume titles: *Ascenseur pour Nulle Part*, *Veuillez Patienter*, *Le Département des Petits Mystères*, *Musique pour les Gens Curieux*, *Très Dramatique, Très Ordinaire*.

Saved melodrama naming candidates: *The Galactic Melodrama Library*, *The Midnight Transmission Archives*, *The Sonoran Sound Archives*, *The Department of Strange Frequencies*, *The Melodramatic Recordings*.

## Catalog conventions (provisional)
KPG-LOR-0001 (lore), KPG-THG-0001 (thing), KPGR-FEM-001 (French Elevator Music), KPGR-MDA-001 (Melodrama). IDs are provisional and should not imply completed releases.
