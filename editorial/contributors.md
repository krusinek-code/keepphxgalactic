# Contributors

Invite Phoenix artists, musicians, and residents to share an unusual experience, an original fictional story, artwork, or sound. Participation is optional and credited.

## Before publishing
1. Agree on contribution scope and whether it is fact, testimony, or fiction.
2. Confirm preferred credit, links, and whether anonymity is requested.
3. Obtain explicit permission for the proposed platform(s), edits, promotion, and reuse.
4. Clarify ownership and music/art licensing; do not imply an open-ended license.
5. Give contributors a chance to review the final attribution and presentation.
