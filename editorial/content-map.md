## Collection concepts

- Midnight Transmissions: broadcast records → witnesses → regional mythology.
- Original transmissions: guest fiction → artwork → soundtrack → follow-on episode.

## Evidence and confidence
Keep **evidence type** separate from **claim confidence**. Evidence types: original observation, primary document, secondary source, testimony, fiction. Editorial status: documented, observed, reported, folklore, interpretation, fictional. These are labels, not a numerical probability. A photographed place does not prove associated legends.
